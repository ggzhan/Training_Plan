# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Any trainer running a Stützpunkt table-tennis session for kids. They open the
Trainingsplaner in the hall, on their phone, usually just before or during
training while kids are arriving, warming up or asking questions. Attention is
split, one hand is often busy, and the plan has to be usable in a minute or two.

## Product Purpose
Turn the kids marked for today's date in the club's Google Sheet into a
sequence of exercises: pairs of similar strength, Balleimer stations and
sparring partners, with no kid repeating a partner, a Balleimer or a sparring
partner. Success means the trainer can call out "Übung 3" and read who goes
where at a glance, and fix the plan on the spot when someone is missing or
arrives late.

## Brand Personality
Practical, calm, sporty. A tool a trainer trusts in a noisy hall, not a
dashboard. German throughout, in the words trainers already use (Übung,
Balleimer, Sparring, Klassierung).

## Anti-references
- Admin dashboards and SaaS settings pages: walls of fields before the
  useful part.
- Decorative gradients and hover effects that do nothing on a phone.

## Design Principles
- The hall comes first: every main action reachable with a thumb, readable at
  arm's length.
- Today's plan in as few taps as possible; settings stay out of the way once
  set.
- Show who goes where, not how the plan was computed.
- Fixing the plan live (late arrival, no-show, swap) is as easy as making it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast and touch targets of at least 44px, since it is used in bright
halls on phones. No specific known user needs beyond that.
